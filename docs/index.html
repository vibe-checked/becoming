Support

How can we help?

Have a question about Becoming or run into an issue? Start with the answers below, or reach out directly — we read every email.

Is Becoming free?
Yes — every feature is included at no cost, with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
Do I need an account to use Becoming?
No. Becoming works entirely without sign-in — just open the app and start a session.
Where are my custom affirmations, photos, and voice recordings stored?
Everything stays on your device. Custom affirmations and settings are stored locally, and photos and voice recordings are saved to the app's own storage on your phone.
How do I add my own photos or affirmations?
Long-press any theme tile on the home screen to open its editor, where you can add photos, write affirmations, and record your own voice.
How does the streak work?
Completing a session on a given day counts toward your streak. Miss a single day and a monthly streak freeze automatically covers it once, so one busy day won't reset weeks of progress.
Can I turn off notifications?
Yes — reminders are entirely optional and can be changed or turned off from within the app at any time.
I found a bug or have a feature idea — who do I tell?
Email mark@vibecode.review — as a one-person project, feedback goes straight to the person building it.

Still need help?

Email us and include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting an issue — it helps us track it down faster.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy, simply explained

Last updated: August 15, 2026. This policy applies to the Becoming iOS application published by Mark Utility Labs.

🔒

Short version: Becoming stores everything — your affirmations, photos, voice recordings, and settings — locally on your device. Nothing is collected, tracked, or sent to a server.

1. What Becoming stores

Custom affirmations, photos you add, voice recordings, session history, streaks, and reminder settings are all created and stored locally on your device.

2. How it's stored

All of this data lives in the app's local storage on your device — there's no cloud account and no server sync. Deleting the app removes all of this data permanently.

3. What we don't collect

Becoming has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no ad identifiers, and no user accounts, and it makes no network requests containing your personal data. We cannot see your affirmations, photos, or recordings.

4. Permissions

Becoming may request Microphone access to record your own voice for custom affirmations, Photo Library access to add your own photos to a theme, and Speech Recognition, used only to support voice recording. You can deny or revoke any of these in iOS Settings without affecting the rest of the app.

5. Third-party content

Some built-in theme photos are sourced from Unsplash, a third-party photo service; these images are fetched for display only and don't involve sending your personal data anywhere. Reminders are scheduled entirely on-device via iOS and are not routed through any push notification server.

6. Children's privacy

Becoming is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the “Last updated” date above.
